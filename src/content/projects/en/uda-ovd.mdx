Master's research, in progress. Unsupervised domain adaptation for
open-vocabulary object detection: a source-free, lightweight adaptation of the
text side of a frozen open-vocabulary detector (Grounding DINO), through
prompt tuning and LoRA. It is also an empirical study of when VLM anchors
actually help pseudo-labelling under domain shift.

The original idea — using CLIP as a filter for pseudo-labels — did not work,
for structural reasons: a recall ceiling, and the anchor's score being
correlated with the teacher's confidence under corruption. That negative
result became the motivation and the analysis, rather than being buried.

Benchmarks are Cityscapes and Foggy Cityscapes. Everything is designed to run
on a single consumer GPU.
