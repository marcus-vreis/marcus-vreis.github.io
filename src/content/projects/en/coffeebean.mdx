Implementation and evaluation of several neural-network models on a
coffee-bean image dataset, for a computer-vision course. The work produced a
written paper documenting methodology, results and discussion, plus an
explanatory video.

The repository is deliberately transparent about the process, so the work can
be reviewed and replicated.
