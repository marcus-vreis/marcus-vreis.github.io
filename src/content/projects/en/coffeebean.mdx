Experimental study on automated coffee-bean quality assessment using computer vision, started in course SIN 393 at [UFV](https://www.crp.ufv.br/) and expanded into an undergraduate research project and B.Sc. thesis advised by Prof. Dr. Pedro Moises de Sousa.

The pipeline applies local contrast enhancement (CLAHE) and multiresolution Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) decomposition prior to classification with convolutional neural networks and ensemble voting in PyTorch. These experiments produced the first-author paper [*Quality Assessment of Coffee Beans Using Convolutional Neural Networks with Wavelet and CLAHE Techniques*](https://doi.org/10.5753/wsis.2024.33669), published in the *Proceedings of the Information Systems Workshop (WSIS 2024, pp. 33–38)*. The [GitHub repository](https://github.com/marcus-vreis/ComputerVision-CoffeeBean) contains the training notebooks, evaluated checkpoints, and [video presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mVWqjKUZZsI).
