Experimental study on automated coffee-bean quality assessment via computer
vision and deep learning, initiated in SIN 393 (UFV) and expanded into
undergraduate research and a B.Sc. thesis advised by Prof. Dr. Pedro Moises de
Sousa.

The pipeline pairs Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalisation (**CLAHE**)
and multiresolution **Discrete Wavelet Transform** decomposition with systematic
training and evaluation of convolutional architectures and **ensemble** methods
in PyTorch. This research line produced the first-author paper published in the
*Proceedings of the Information Systems Workshop (WSIS 2024, pp. 33–38)* —
*Quality Assessment of Coffee Beans Using Convolutional Neural Networks with
Wavelet and CLAHE Techniques* — alongside the full paper and walkthrough video
in the repository.
