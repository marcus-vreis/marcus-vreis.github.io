Applying and evaluating k-NN and SVM on a modified MPEG7 shape dataset. It
covers the whole classical pipeline: image preprocessing and filtering with
scikit-image, feature extraction and selection, then classification.

Performance is measured by accuracy, precision, recall and F1.
