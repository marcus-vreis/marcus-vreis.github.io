A complete classical visual pattern-recognition pipeline on a modified
**MPEG7** silhouette benchmark. The study walks through every pre-deep-learning
stage: filtering and morphological segmentation with `scikit-image`, shape and
region descriptor extraction, feature selection and feature-space projection.

At the decision stage, it contrasts non-parametric neighbourhood boundaries
(**k-Nearest Neighbors**) against maximum-margin classifiers (**Support Vector
Machines**), measuring sensitivity to $k$, kernel hyperparameters, and
per-class accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
