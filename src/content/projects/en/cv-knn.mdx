Classical shape-recognition pipeline evaluated on a subset of the MPEG7 silhouette benchmark. The code in [`ComputerVision-KNN`](https://github.com/marcus-vreis/ComputerVision-KNN) performs morphological preprocessing and segmentation with `scikit-image`, extracts contour and region descriptors, and applies feature selection and projection prior to classification.

The evaluation compares local neighbourhood decision boundaries (*k-Nearest Neighbors*) against maximum-margin classifiers (*Support Vector Machines* in `scikit-learn`), measuring the sensitivity of accuracy, precision, recall, and per-class F1-score to the choice of $k$ and kernel parameters.
