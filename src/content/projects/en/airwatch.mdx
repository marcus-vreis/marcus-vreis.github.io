A distributed system for collecting, aggregating and visualising air-quality
data from simulated sensors. Multiple collector nodes batch their readings
and send them to a central aggregator, which is consistent and tolerant of
duplicates.

Results are exposed live over REST and WebSocket, and drawn into a dashboard.
The whole system runs under Docker Compose. Built for a distributed-systems
course.
