A distributed system for collecting, aggregating and visualising air-quality
telemetry across multiple collector nodes, built for the Distributed Systems
course (SIN 142, UFV — Prof. Dr. Pedro Damaso).

The architecture decouples collection from ingestion through **two RabbitMQ
queues**: the central node dispatches collection requests on the first queue;
worker nodes poll the external source, batch the readings into a single payload
and publish it onto the second queue tagged with a unique idempotency token.
The central core ingests batches with duplicate tolerance and persists the
consolidated state.

Downstream, processed aggregates stream live over **WebSocket** and remain
queryable over a **REST API**, feeding a multi-group sensor dashboard. The
entire cluster (collectors, RabbitMQ broker, core and frontend) boots
reproducibly under `docker-compose`.
