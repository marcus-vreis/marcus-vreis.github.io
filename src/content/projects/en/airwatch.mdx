[AirWatch](https://github.com/marcus-vreis/AirWatch) collects, aggregates, and streams air-quality readings from multiple collector nodes. The system was built in TypeScript for the Distributed Systems course (SIN 142) at the [Federal University of Viçosa (UFV)](https://www.crp.ufv.br/), taught by Prof. Dr. Pedro Damaso.

Two RabbitMQ queues decouple collection from central aggregation. The coordinator publishes polling jobs to the first queue; collector nodes query external sensors, batch the readings, and publish the payload to the second queue with a batch idempotency token. The core service deduplicates redelivered batches and updates the consolidated state.

Processed aggregates stream live over WebSockets and remain queryable through a REST API. The entire topology (collectors, RabbitMQ broker, aggregation core, and web dashboard) runs via `docker-compose`.
