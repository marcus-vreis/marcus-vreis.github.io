A desktop GUI application for classical digital image processing, built for a
DIP course at UFV. Deliberately modular: the interface is separated from the
processing logic, with one module per family of operation.

It covers histogram operations, intensity transforms (contrast stretching,
equalisation), spatial filters (low-pass and high-pass), the frequency domain
via Fourier, morphology (erosion and dilation), and Otsu thresholding.
