A desktop workbench for hands-on classical Digital Image Processing (SIN 392,
UFV). Engineered as a pixel-level inspection instrument: the GUI and event loop
(`main.py`) are strictly separated from the six pure mathematical processing
modules.

Each transformation family lives in its own dedicated module:
`operacoes_histograma.py` (tonal distribution and statistics),
`transformacoes_intensidade.py` (contrast stretching and histogram
equalisation), `filtros_espaciais.py` (low-pass smoothing and high-pass
edge/sharpening convolutions), `dominio_frequencia.py` (spectral analysis and
filtering via Fast Fourier Transform), `operacoes_morfologicas.py`
(structuring-element erosion and dilation) and `operacoes_segmentacao.py`
(global optimal Otsu thresholding).
