[ImageLab](https://github.com/marcus-vreis/ImageLab) is a desktop application built for the Digital Image Processing course (SIN 392) at [UFV](https://www.crp.ufv.br/). The codebase isolates GUI layout and event dispatch (`main.py`) from six pure mathematical processing modules.

Each transformation family lives in its own module. `operacoes_histograma.py` computes tonal distributions and global statistics, while `transformacoes_intensidade.py` implements contrast stretching and histogram equalization. Spatial and frequency filtering reside in `filtros_espaciais.py` (low-pass smoothing and high-pass edge operators) and `dominio_frequencia.py` (2D Fourier spectrum visualization and frequency-domain FFT filtering). `operacoes_morfologicas.py` (erosion and dilation with configurable structuring elements) and `operacoes_segmentacao.py` (global optimal thresholding via Otsu's method) complete the pipeline.
