An empirical sorting benchmark suite written in pure C++ (`Algoritmos.h`,
`heap_func.h`), covering `Insertion_Sort`, `Selection_Sort`, `Merge_Sort`,
`Heap_Sort` and four `Quick_Sort` partitioning strategies (standard pivot,
mean, median-of-three and randomised pivot).

Beyond implementing each algorithm from scratch, the repository measures and
records wall-clock execution times across three input distributions
(**ascending**, **descending** and **random**) spanning six orders of magnitude
($N = 10, 10^2, 10^3, 10^4, 10^5, 10^6$), exposing where quadratic $O(N^2)$
degradation surfaces in practice and how median-of-three and randomised pivots
preserve the $O(N \log N)$ regime.
