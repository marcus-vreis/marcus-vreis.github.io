Empirical sorting benchmark written in C++ (`Algoritmos.h` and `heap_func.h`) in [`Sorting-Algorithms-Hub`](https://github.com/marcus-vreis/Sorting-Algorithms-Hub). The repository implements `Insertion_Sort`, `Selection_Sort`, `Merge_Sort`, `Heap_Sort`, and four pivot-selection strategies for `Quick_Sort` (first element, mean, median-of-three, and random pivot).

The benchmark records CPU execution times across three input distributions (ascending, descending, and random) over six orders of magnitude ($N = 10, 10^2, 10^3, 10^4, 10^5, 10^6$). The resulting curves measure the transition to quadratic $O(N^2)$ runtime when fixed pivots encounter pre-sorted arrays and verify $O(N \log N)$ stability under median-of-three and randomized pivoting.
